Our Mission

Ever-changing.
Ever-growing.

Healing doesn’t move in a straight line. Neither does a movement.

Sisters of the Fire is an ever-changing, ever-growing community advocacy and support initiative for women, children, and families affected by domestic violence and life-changing crises.

We’re building connections to essential supplies, educational resources, community support, and qualified legal help. We listen, learn, and grow alongside the people we hope to serve.

Our Story

Born from compassion.
Built on sisterhood.

Sisters of the Fire was inspired by the support and compassion shared during an incredibly difficult moment.

Co-founders Wendy Shumate and Amy Pugh envision a community where survivors are heard, supported, and given practical opportunities to rebuild.

We’re still growing, too. You can be in the middle of your own journey and still believe in a better path for someone else. There’s no finish line you have to cross before you belong.

A Few Things to Know

Your choices.
Your pace.

Do I need to share my story to join?

No. You do not need to describe your experience to follow our work or express volunteer interest. Please do not send case details, addresses, documents, or information about children through website forms.

Are services and volunteer roles available now?

We are building the initiative. Service capacity, volunteer roles, training, and safeguarding procedures are not established yet. Expressing interest will not guarantee services or a volunteer position.

Can I donate goods or money?

Yes. We’re accepting goods donations now, including bags of items you’re ready to pass along. Call or email us to arrange a pickup or a place to meet. We do not have a public drop-off or mailing address.

You can also use Donate Money to visit our Kindbee fundraiser. Sisters of the Fire is not currently represented as a registered 501(c)(3) charity. Donations are not advertised as tax-deductible.

Can you help with legal matters or provide belonging retrieval?

We do not provide legal advice, legal representation, or belonging retrieval services. Any future retrieval support would require lawful, safe arrangements and appropriate professional guidance.

Privacy and safer browsing

Quick exit has limits

Quick exit opens Google in this tab. It does not erase browser history, downloads, device monitoring, or other records. Consider a safer device if someone else may be monitoring your activity.

No signup is needed to read

You can browse our pages without submitting any information. The Sisterhood newsletter is coming soon. We do not add advertising trackers, analytics, or save form entries in browser storage. Your browser, autofill settings, and hosting provider may still keep records. We cannot promise anonymity or confidentiality.

Volunteer and partner interest

If you submit the interest form, your preferred name, email address, selected interest, and permission to contact you are sent to Formspree for processing and storage, and forwarded to Sisters of the Fire at sistersofthefire@yahoo.com. Formspree may also process technical information under its privacy policy. Our email provider handles the delivered email under its own policies.

Only submit an email address where it is safe for us to respond. Do not send case histories, children’s information, private addresses, or sensitive documents. This form is not for urgent help and does not create an account, approve a role, or subscribe you to a newsletter. To ask us to stop contacting you or delete your interest details, email sistersofthefire@yahoo.com .

Donations and contacting us

Donate Money takes you to the Kindbee fundraiser. Donate Goods takes you to our Contact Us section, where you can call or email us to arrange a pickup or a place to meet. Kindbee, Google Voice, and your email or phone provider handle information you choose to send under their own policies. Please do not include case histories, children’s information, addresses, or sensitive documents in an initial email.

Sender names, subjects, and notification previews may reveal a connection to Sisters of the Fire. Choose a safe account and device if you contact us.
